Destination model

Geometry belongs to state.

Position, size, scale and rotation are authored as destination properties. The transition simply interpolates between two complete spatial descriptions.

Anchored position

Destinations can be expressed against edges or centre rather than hard-wired transforms.

Size ≠ scale

Layout width/height and presentation scale remain separate.

Rotation is geometry

Rotate Z/X/Y can interpolate with position and size when Slide is enabled.
