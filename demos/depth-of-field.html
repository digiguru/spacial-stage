3D focus experiment

A plane can have a focal band.

The card is one tilted 3D surface, but its visible output is sliced into narrow strips. Each strip receives blur according to its distance from a focus plane, creating a near-soft → sharp → far-soft depth-of-field effect.

CSS perspective is used as the camera proxy: smaller values exaggerate perspective; larger values flatten it like a longer lens.

Moves the sharp plane from the near edge, through the middle, to the far edge.
