Terminology under test

Focus moved out of “Transition”

Push, reveal and collapse describe what changes. Focus describes perceived depth. The playground keeps them separate so we can see if that feels natural.

“Locking” became “Attachment”

Float, dock and pin describe the object's relationship to layout more clearly than “locking”.

“Interaction” became “Coordination”

Swap, stagger and follow are relationships between moving objects, rather than user interactions.
